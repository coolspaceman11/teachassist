TeachAssist+ — GD Ship Aircraft Balance & Expansion

Changes
=======
- Elite/big missiles are substantially less aggressive: lower initial speed, lower late-game speed multiplier, reduced straight-line catch-up boost, and reduced homing turn rate. They remain 3 HP.
- Boss idle/return position is anchored around the upper-middle of the camera instead of following a point far to the player's right. Boss 2/3 can still briefly leave that area during telegraphed ram attacks, then return.
- Boss countdowns are sequential. Boss 1 waits 30s. After Boss 1 is defeated, Boss 2 waits 60s. After Boss 2 is defeated, Boss 3 waits 120s. After Boss 3 is defeated, Boss 4 waits 300s. A later countdown never runs while the previous boss is alive.
- Survival income restored to $3 per second.

New aircraft (new sprites are used in the exact order supplied)
===============================================================
Scout — $500
- 120 base speed
- compact lower-speed/lower-spread rear flares

Fighter — $4,400
- 232 base speed (3rd fastest base aircraft)
- two homing anti-missile seekers
- no rear-gun cooldown
- rear gun now fires 3 pellets with a small spread

Stealth — $4,000
- 205 base speed
- launches flares from both front and rear
- automatically receives a 1.5s speed boost when 4+ missiles are active
- passive boost has a 5s cooldown

Gunship — $10,000 + 4 lifetime boss defeats
- 238 base speed (2nd highest base speed)
- 3 homing anti-missile seekers
- automatic emergency front/rear flares when a missile gets close
- 7-round rapid minigun burst as the front boss cannon
- no rear-gun cooldown
- rear gun fires 3 pellets
- same automatic 4+ missile speed boost as Stealth
- lifetime boss defeats persist across runs

Existing weapon balance
=======================
- All rear guns now fire 3 pellets with a small spread.
- Jet/Interceptor retain their normal rear-gun cooldown.
- Fighter/Gunship rear gun is unrestricted by a cooldown.
- Bomber seeker cooldown is now 1.5s.
- Fighter launches 2 seekers per use; Gunship launches 3.

Developer testing
=================
DEVTEST now includes Scout, Fighter, Stealth, and Gunship in the unlock-all set.

No new native dependencies.
